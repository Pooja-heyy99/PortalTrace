How It Works

Step 1: Register

Submit product details (name, manufacturer, origin, IPFS metadata). The contract generates a unique product ID and stores it on-chain.

Step 2: Transfer

As products move through the supply chain, authorized parties can transfer ownership, maintaining a permanent record of custody.

Step 3: Verify

End consumers and retailers can scan a QR code or enter the product ID to instantly verify authenticity and view the complete ownership history.
